All commands work two ways: type **`?command`** in any text channel, or use the slash command **`/command`**. Slash commands are easier — they show you the options as you type.

**Getting started**
- **1.** Join a voice channel yourself, then run **`/join`** and pick that channel from the list.
- **2.** Run **`?add`** with a YouTube link to put a song in the queue.
  `?add https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ`
- **3.** Run **`?play`**. The first song in the queue starts.

Add as many links as you like, before or during playback. They line up in a **queue** and play in order.
Run **`?help`** at any time to see this list again.

**Voice channel**
- **`?join`** ***<voice channel>*** — Brings the bot into a voice channel. Use **`/join`** and pick from the list; **`?join`** needs the channel's ID, which is harder to find.
- **`?leave`** — The bot disconnects but remembers the queue. Run **`?join`** again to carry on where you left off.
- **`?stop`** — Stops the music, disconnects, and empties the queue.

**Playback**
- **`?add`** ***<YouTube link>*** — Adds a song to the end of the queue. Paste a **playlist** link and every song in it is added at once.
- **`?play`** — Starts playing from the top of the queue.
- **`?pause`** — Pauses the music. **`?resume`** continues from the exact same spot.
- **`?skip`** — Jumps to the next song in the queue.
- **`?skipto`** ***<number>*** — Jumps straight to a song further down the queue. Run **`?queue`** first to see the numbers.
  `?skipto 5`

**Queue**
The queue is the list of songs waiting to play, in order. Everyone in the server shares the same one.
- **`?queue`** — Shows the first 10 songs. The song playing right now is marked *(Playing)*.
- **`?queueto`** ***<page>*** — Shows a later page of the queue, 10 songs per page.
  `?queueto 3`
- **`?clear`** — Empties the queue. The song playing right now is not interrupted.

**Volume**
- **`?volume`** ***<0 to 100>*** — Sets how loud the bot plays for everyone. It starts at **10**, and the change is heard straight away — no need to restart the song.
  `?volume 25`

**Notes**
- The bot leaves on its own after about a minute with nothing playing, so it does not sit in your channel all day.
- Songs that are private, deleted, or blocked in your country are skipped automatically, and the bot tells you which ones.
- If a song fails to load, it is tried once more on a fresh link before being skipped.
- Adding many links quickly may get you a short "slow down" message. Nothing is lost — wait a few seconds and try again.
