**Music from YouTube, straight into your voice channel.** Three steps to start — the menu below has everything else.

**🚀 Getting started**
### Play something now
- **1.** Join a voice channel, then run **`/join`** and pick it from the list.
- **2.** Run **`/add`** and paste a YouTube link.
  `/add https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ`
- **3.** Run **`/play`**.

A **Now playing** message appears with **⏸ Pause** and **⏭ Skip** buttons on it. That's the whole thing.

### `/play` and `?play` both work
**`/play`** shows you the command and fills in the options as you type, so there is nothing to memorise. **`?play`** is the same command typed straight into chat, and it's quicker once you know the name. This manual writes **`?`** to keep things short.

### Where to go next
**Queue** — the songs lined up behind this one.
**Playlists** — save songs once with **`?playlist`**, and start the lot with one command every time after.

**🎵 Playing music**
### Getting the bot in and out
- **`?join`** ***<channel>*** — Use **`/join`** and pick from the list; the **`?`** form needs a channel ID, which is harder to find.
- **`?leave`** — Disconnects, but remembers the queue.
- **`?stop`** — Stops, empties the queue, and leaves.

### While it's playing
- **`?add`** ***<YouTube link>*** — Adds a song to the queue. A **playlist** link adds every song in it at once.
- **`?play`** — Starts from the top of the queue.
- **`?pause`** — Pauses, and shows exactly where it stopped. **`?resume`** picks up from that spot. Leave it paused for about a minute and the bot disconnects on its own.
- **`?skip`** — Jumps to the next song, and says who did.
- **`?nowplaying`** — What's playing, how far in it is, what's next, and which playlist it came from.
- **`?volume`** ***<0 to 100>*** — Starts at **10**, and the change is heard straight away.
  `?volume 25`

**📜 Queue**
The queue is the line of songs waiting to play. Everyone in the server shares it, and it empties when the bot stops.

- **`?queue`** — Shows it. The song playing right now is marked **▶**.
- **`?queueto`** ***<page>*** — Opens it at a later page, 10 songs each.
  `?queueto 3`
- **`?skipto`** ***<number>*** — Jumps straight to a song further down. Run **`?queue`** first to see the numbers.
- **`?clear`** — Empties it. The song playing right now is not interrupted.

-# **◀ ▶** page through · the **page number** between them jumps straight to any page · **🔄** re-reads where you are, since songs finish and people add more while you're looking.

**💾 Playlists**
A playlist is songs you save once and start again with one command. It belongs to **this server** — everyone here can add to it, play it, and it's still there after a restart.

### Make one
- **1.** **`?playlist create Late Night`**
- **2.** **`?playlist add "Late Night" <YouTube link>`** — a whole YouTube playlist link works too.
- **3.** **`?playlist play Late Night`**

From then on, step 3 is the only one you need.

### Or skip the typing
Run **`?playlist`** on its own, pick one from the menu, and press **▶ Play** or **➕ Add to queue**. Long ones page with **◀ ▶**, or tap the page number to jump.

### Every command
- **`?playlist`** — All of them, with the picker. The one playing right now is marked **▶**.
- **`?playlist create`** ***<name>*** — A new, empty one.
- **`?playlist add`** ***<name> <link>*** — Adds a song, or a whole YouTube playlist.
- **`?playlist show`** ***<name>*** — What's inside, 10 songs per page.
- **`?playlist remove`** ***<name> <number>*** — Takes one song out, by the number **`?playlist show`** prints.
- **`?playlist play`** ***<name>*** — **Replaces** the queue and starts it.
- **`?playlist queue`** ***<name>*** — **Keeps** the queue and adds to the end. Tells you which song it opens with and how long until you hear it.
- **`?playlist rename`** ***<old> <new>*** and **`?playlist delete`** ***<name>*** — Its creator, or **Manage Server**.

### Good to know
Anyone here can add and remove **songs**. Only whoever made a playlist, or a moderator, can rename or delete the **playlist itself**.
Names with spaces need quotes on **`add`**, **`remove`** and **`rename`** — or use **`/playlist`**, which needs none and lists the names as you type.
Playing one **copies** it into the queue, so **`?clear`** and **`?skip`** never change the playlist. Up to 25 per server, 10,000 songs each.

**🎛️ Buttons**
Every **Now playing** message comes with buttons, so the things you do most need no typing at all.
- **⏸ Pause** — pauses, and turns into **▶ Resume**. The message updates in place and says who paused it.
- **⏭ Skip** — jumps to the next song, and says who pressed it.
- **📜 Queue** — opens the queue where only you can see it.

You have to be in the voice channel to use them, because they change what everyone hears. They go grey when the song ends or after about 15 minutes. The message stops updating at the same time, so an old one shows where the song had got to rather than pretending to be live. The commands always work.

**💡 Notes**
- The bot leaves on its own after about a minute with nothing playing, so it doesn't sit in your channel all day.
- Songs that are private, deleted, or blocked in your country are skipped automatically, and the bot says which ones.
- If a song fails to load, it's tried once more on a fresh link before being skipped.
- Adding lots of links quickly may get you a short "slow down" message. Nothing is lost — wait a few seconds.
